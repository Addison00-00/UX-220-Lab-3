Under the meticulous and visionary direction of Haruo Sotozaki, Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba—The Movie: Infinity Castle (劇場版「鬼滅の刃」無限城編) is a Japanese animated dark fantasy epic-action film based on the "Infinity Castle" arc of the Demon Slayer manga series by Koyoharu Gotouge. It follows the climactic story-telling of the Demon Slayer Corps (a secret, unofficial and unrecognized covert organization of swordsmen) and their intense battles against Muzan Kibutsuji—the main antagonist and the first and most powerful demon—and the remaining Upper Ranks Demons in the endless “Infinity Castle”.
